## Key Takeaways

### Storage Engines
- **Hash indexes**: Good for exact matches, O(1) lookup
- **B-Trees**: Most common, good for reads and writes
- **LSM-Trees**: Excellent write performance, good for write-heavy workloads

### OLTP vs OLAP
- OLTP: Transaction-oriented, many small operations
- OLAP: Analytics-oriented, few complex queries
- Data warehousing bridges the gap

### Column-Oriented Storage
- Better for analytics workloads
- Better compression
- Enables vectorized processing
